About Wonderprints

We turn life’s biggest moments into keepsakes people are happy to hold on to.

Our story

Small details, lasting memories

Wonderprints is a personalized souvenir and giveaway studio. We believe the best celebrations are remembered through the little things guests take home: a name, a date, a thoughtful design.

Whether you are planning a wedding for two hundred guests or a baptism for a close circle of family, we give every order the same care. We listen first, design second, and produce with attention to every finish.

Our champagne-and-gold look reflects what we hope each piece feels like: warm, refined and made to be cherished.
